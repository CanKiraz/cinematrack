# CinemaTrack 0.2.1

The third test release. Everything in it was asked for by testers: a readable sort menu in dark theme, sorting by date added on the
phone, a proper place for account settings on Android, and changing your display name.

## Download

- **Windows:** `CinemaTrack Setup 0.2.1.exe`
- **Android:** `CinemaTrack-0.2.1.apk`

Both are not code-signed yet, so Windows shows a "protected your PC" warning (More info → Run anyway) and Android asks you to allow
installing from this source. Step-by-step help is in the [tester guide](../tester-guide.md).

**Updating:** install over the old version; your account and lists stay.

## What's new

- **Fixed (Android): the sort menu was unreadable in the phone's dark theme.** It is now always readable, whatever theme the phone uses.
- **Sort by date added (Android).** *Newest added* and *Oldest added* join the sort menu. The date is now stored in the cloud, so it is the
  same on the PC and the phone. Titles you added before this version get their date the first time the desktop app runs
  (open the desktop app once, then the phone sees it); anything without a date is listed last.
- **A settings gear on Android**, next to the EN/TR button. *Sign out* and *Delete account* moved there from the Statistics page.
- **Change your display name** on both apps (desktop: Settings → *Change name*; phone: the gear).
- **Display names are checked.** Names with swear words, slurs or impersonating names such as "admin" are refused when you sign up or rename.
  Existing accounts keep working; a name that would not pass today is shown as "User" to the AI.

## Good to know

- You need an account with a **real email address**. Your lists and notes are private to you.
- The AI features have daily limits (for example 30 chat messages and 10 Watch Together picks per person).
- The name check needs the internet; if it cannot be reached, your name is accepted as it is.
