# CinemaTrack 0.1.0: first test release

Track the movies and series you watch on your PC and your phone, and let an AI help you pick, alone or together with someone.
This is an early version for testers: **your feedback decides what we fix next.**

## Download

- **Windows:** `CinemaTrack Setup 0.1.0.exe`
- **Android:** `CinemaTrack-0.1.0.apk`

Both are not code-signed yet, so Windows shows a "protected your PC" warning (More info → Run anyway) and Android asks you to
allow installing from this source. Step-by-step help is in the [tester guide](../tester-guide.md).

## What you can do

- Search movies and series, keep a **watchlist**, mark things as **watched** with a rating and a note.
- See your **statistics** and a calendar of what you watched.
- Ask the **AI chat** what to watch tonight. It knows your list and your history.
- **Watch Together:** two people join a session with a 6-letter code, each says what they feel like, and the AI picks one title for both.
- On Android, **share an Instagram reel** to CinemaTrack and it finds the movie.
- Everything stays in sync between your PC and your phone. English and Turkish.

## Good to know

- You need an account with a **real email address** (we send a confirmation link). Your lists and notes are private to you.
- The AI features have daily limits (for example 30 chat messages and 10 Watch Together picks per person), so the free services last for everyone.
- The Instagram lookup is best effort: it works best when the caption names the movie.

## What changed from the earlier private builds

No API keys inside the apps any more (they live on a server), private lists, Watch Together that shares only a short summary of titles and ratings,
real email accounts, and a test-covered set of security rules. The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
